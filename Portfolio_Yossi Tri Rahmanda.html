Occupational Safety Incident (LTI) Prediction — Coal Mining

Python · Google Colab

End-to-end analysis of 2,699 SHE (Safety, Health, Environment) incident records from PT Banana Coal covering 2022–2024: data collection & validation, cleaning inconsistent formats (area names, shifts, working-hour outliers), feature engineering, and building a classification model to predict Lost Time Injury (LTI) events before they happen.

Key insight: After removing data leakage (dropping variables only known after an incident occurred), the Logistic Regression model showed limited performance due to class imbalance — LTI cases are far outnumbered by non-LTI incidents. The most influential factors were work areas with non-standardized ("unknown") logging, specific Pit blocks, and certain supervisors — so my top recommendation is standardizing area logging and running targeted audits on the highest-risk blocks/supervisors, rather than relying on model accuracy alone.

Hotel Room Price Prediction & Time Series Forecasting

R · RStudio

Built several modeling approaches to predict hotel average price per room: multiple linear regression (handling multicollinearity by removing high-VIF variables & applying a log transformation), Decision Tree, Random Forest, and an ARIMA time series model to project upcoming daily price trends.

Key insight: Random Forest substantially outperformed a single Decision Tree (MAPE 5.70% vs 26.33%) by capturing non-linear relationships among room-price variables more reliably. For short-term projections, the ARIMA(2,1,3) model — selected automatically via auto.arima() — delivered solid accuracy (MAPE ≈ 20.8%) and projects prices stabilizing around 200 with no sharp upward or downward trend. Residual diagnostics confirm the model is well-specified: residuals hover around zero with no significant autocorrelation and an approximately normal distribution — meaning the forecast captures the underlying pattern rather than leftover noise.
